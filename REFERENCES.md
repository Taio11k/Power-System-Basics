| **[IEC60364]** | IEC 60364 (low-voltage installations) | Building wiring. Learn it through **[EIG]** |
| **[EN50160]** | EN 50160 *Voltage characteristics of electricity supplied by public distribution systems* | Supply voltage within ±10 % for 95 % of the 10-minute mean values over one week. [Schneider technical note](https://www.productinfo.schneider-electric.com/pm8000/59834d6446e0fb0001420311/Technical%20note/BM_TechnicalNoteEN50160_0000153756.ditamap/$/C_PQ_EN50160SupplyVoltageVariations_0000153762) |

## G. Job-market evidence (why the course is built this way)

Graduate power systems engineer postings ask for load flow and fault level studies, dynamics and transients, PowerFactory / PSCAD / PSS®E / MATLAB, Python scripting, and **written study reports**. Examples: [TARGETjobs: Graduate Electrical Power Systems Engineer](https://targetjobs.co.uk/jobs/graduate-electrical-power-systems-engineer-216306), [StudentJob UK: Graduate Power Systems Engineer](https://www.studentjob.co.uk/vacancies/6030631-graduate-power-systems-engineer), [DIgSILENT Australia careers](https://mail.digsilent.com.au/pages/careers/position_details?id=5). Postings change over time, so read 10 current ones in your own target country during Phase 0.
