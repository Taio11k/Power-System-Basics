## 6. Vocabulary to own before the call (and the next round)

**Documents and QA:** ITP (Inspection and Test Plan: what will be tested and witnessed) · ITR (Inspection and Test Record: the signed result sheet per item) · test pack (ITRs grouped per system or area) · MDR (Manufacturer's Data Report, the handover dossier) · as-builts / redlines · RFI · NCR · variation and EOT · lookahead · hold point and witness point · FAT / SAT · punch list / defects · practical completion · PIMS (the completions database where ITRs are entered).

**Installation:** cable schedule · cable ladder/tray · gland and termination · loop check · megger (insulation resistance) · continuity · earth continuity and earth-fault loop impedance · polarity · MCC · VSD · switchroom · 3.3 kV / 11 kV / 415 V · arc-fault containment · LOTO, isolation and permit to work · HV switching.

**Standards (quote the number, not the content):** AS/NZS 3000:2018 Wiring Rules · AS/NZS 3008.1.1:2017 cable selection (current-carrying capacity, voltage drop, short-circuit temperature rise; Australian conditions 40 °C air / 25 °C soil) · AS/NZS 61439 series (LV switchgear and controlgear assemblies) · **AS/NZS 5368:2025 Electrical equipment in mines and quarries** (new in 2025; supersedes AS/NZS 3007:2013 and the AS/NZS 4871 series; knowing it exists will land with an engineering manager) · NSW Resources Regulator code of practice *Electrical engineering control plan* (EECP), May 2025, under the WHS (Mines and Petroleum Sites) legislation · AS 2067 substations and HV installations exceeding 1 kV (confirm the current edition on Standards Australia before quoting it).

Practice sentence: "On an MCC replacement I'd expect an ITP with hold points at pre-energisation IR and continuity tests, ITRs per cubicle and per cable, and all of it compiled into the MDR for Newmont at handover."

## 9. After the screen: the hiring-manager round

PHE's process says step 3 is a one-on-one interview or group session, possibly followed by a second interview. Expect an engineering or project manager from Bathurst, 30–60 minutes, Teams or in person, with some technical conversation. Prepare:

1. **Cable sizing walkthrough:** size a 415 V motor feeder in words with AS/NZS 3008.1.1 logic (load current, derating for installation method and grouping, voltage drop limit, short-circuit withstand). Pull course Step 2.3 forward this month.
2. **Fault level and switchgear rating:** why a 1997 board fails arc-fault containment, what a fault-level study is for, how IEC 60909 gives the peak and breaking currents a board must be rated for. Course Phase 4; read the Step 4.1 Learn section early.
3. **Safety:** isolation and LOTO, permit to work, why HV switching is a licensed activity, the EECP as the mine's electrical safety framework.
4. **Commissioning sequence:** mechanical completion → pre-commissioning (IR, continuity, loop checks, ITRs signed) → energisation → functional tests → handover with MDR.
5. **Bring one page:** the Nephros schematic (clean export), an I/O list, and how you tested it. It is the closest thing you have to a site deliverable. A second page with a CSV Plotter screenshot shows you can read PSCAD/PSS/E output, which the Engineering division will notice.
